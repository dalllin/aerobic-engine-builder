WHAT THE PLAN COVERS

Aerobic work and whole-body support

Strength sessions combine squat or split-squat, hip-hinge, push, pull, and core movements. Calf, ankle, and hip stability work adds support around common running movements.

This is an educational template, not a personalized medical plan. Scale it to what you currently tolerate, and take extra rest when needed.
