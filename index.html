YOUR 14-DAY PLAN

A plan built around your week

Sets, reps, effort, and progression
  • Strength: Use the sets, rep range, rest, and reps-in-reserve shown on each session. Finish each set with a few good reps still possible; no maximum lifts are needed.
  • Progress: When all sets reach the top of the rep range with controlled form for two sessions, add a small amount of resistance or use a slightly harder variation. Change one thing at a time.
  • Aerobic effort: Easy sessions should let you speak in full sentences. Threshold work is controlled and comfortably hard. Faster intervals are optional and should remain repeatable.
  • Recovery: Rest days are part of the plan. If you miss a session, resume with the next planned day instead of cramming workouts together.

General adult guidance is 150–300 minutes of moderate aerobic activity each week and muscle-strengthening activity for major muscle groups on at least 2 days. This planner can start below that level and build toward it over time.

THE TRAINING MIX

Aerobic work plus whole-body support

Sessions can include easy endurance, threshold work, VO₂-style intervals, short fast efforts, and strength for legs, hips, back, chest, shoulders, arms, and trunk.

The joint-support choices select nearby muscle and balance work; they do not diagnose or treat pain or injury. Stop any movement that causes sharp or increasing pain and seek qualified advice for an injury or medical condition.
